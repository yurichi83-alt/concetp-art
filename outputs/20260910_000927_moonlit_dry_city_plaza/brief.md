# 달빛 아래 마른 시가지 광장

- 사용자 원문: 이런 느낌의 시가지였으면 좋겠는데 바닥의 물의 비치는 부분을 제외하고 첨부한 이미지를 분석해서 키워드를 추출 한 다음에 지금 우리가 구축한 메인 레퍼런스랑 이미지 생성 룰을 도입해서 이미지를 생성해줘
- 유형: 첨부 이미지의 공간과 분위기를 참고한 신규 생성. 최종 한 장.
- 기준: 마스터 01~04 v2.1 / 생성 실행 규칙 v1.2. 이번 해석으로 마스터를 갱신하지 않는다.

## 추출 키워드

넓은 내부 석재 광장 / 주거·소상점의 연속 건물군 / 비대칭 건물 폭과 깊이 / 중앙 뒤 건물과 낮은 주변 건물의 위계 / 다양한 지붕선 / 뒤쪽 양면의 아치 출구와 짧은 계단 / 청회색 달빛 / 제한된 따뜻한 창과 벽등 / 건물 하단의 옅은 안개 / 외곽 생활·수리 흔적 / 두꺼운 기초와 노출 설비.

핵심 차이는 도로 교차점보다 여러 건물이 둘러싼 광장에 가깝다는 점이다. 외곽 건물의 구성 밀도를 살리고 중앙은 넓고 비워 둔다.

## 현재 마스터로 번역

- 물웅덩이와 반사: 제거. 큰 포장석의 건조한 표면과 확산광으로 표현.
- 높고 잘린 건물: 낮은 1~2층의 연결 건물군으로 재설계하고 전체 프레임 여유를 확보.
- 전면 건물/트럭: 가림을 줄이도록 전면 건물은 낮은 측면 조각으로 제한. 차량은 사용하지 않음.
- 작은 돌·박락의 전면 반복: LDI 보강 이미지의 넓은 색면, 선택적인 큰 파손과 수리판으로 정리.
- 세계관: 회수 전력 모듈의 우회 공급, 기계 결합부를 재사용한 구조 보강, 재활용 외함의 일상 설비 등 건축의 결손과 보수 관계를 큰 형태로 설계.
- 지면: 아치 출구까지의 짧은 계단과 참을 연결하고 중앙은 하나의 연속 포장 공간으로 유지.
- 단면: 광장 공용 배수·펌프와 건물 공급 전력의 다른 기능을 양면에 설계. 지하 이동 통로는 만들지 않음.
- 기존 생성물의 기계 비중은 상대적 존재감으로 참고하며 장치·단면 패턴을 복제하지 않음.

## 도구에 전달할 생성 지시

Use case: stylized-concept.
Create ONE new game-environment concept image: a moonlit old CITY SQUARE enclosed by a varied group of buildings in a declining SALVAGE CYBERPUNK world. The user explicitly wants the spatial feeling of image 1, with ALL wet-ground reflections removed, rendered under the project masters described below. This is a newly designed scene using references, not an exact copy or a polish of the previous street-junction image.

INPUT ROLES AND PRIORITY:
Image 1, user's chosen generated example: use its broad enclosed stone PLAZA, many adjoining building facades of varied widths and silhouettes around the perimeter, layered roofline, two rear arched passages reached by short stairs, cold night/warm window contrast and thin ground mist. Do NOT copy its wet ground, mirror reflections, microscopic stonework/weathering, tall foreground buildings, excessive building height, edge cropping, words/posters or truck. It is a scene-and-mood reference subordinate to the spatial and art masters.
Image 2, Master02: use ONLY the connectivity diagram: two adjacent rear boundaries, exactly ONE visible exit per boundary, unobstructed center. Do not render the diagram's blue walls, icons, arrows or text.
Image 3, Master01: use ONLY the complete standalone quarter-view base and exposed two camera-facing foundation sides. Exclude neon, Japanese signs, street markings, color and fine material detail.
Image 4, approved Little Devil Inside Master03 architectural supplement: the strongest MATERIAL AND SHAPE rendering reference. Broad calm plaster color planes, substantial window trim, large paving units, readable architectural volumes and coherent cast-light shapes. Exclude people, plants, signs, game universe, camera and pub theme.
Image 5, Master04: use chunky salvaged advanced-machine housings, joints and mismatched replacement panels as a design language translated into BUILDING SERVICES. Exclude the character, literal giant arms and photographic surface detail. The world remains Salvage Cyberpunk, never LDI's universe.

SCENE DESIGN:
A wide open stone civic plaza framed by a connected, asymmetrical ensemble of low old residential and small service buildings. Distinct narrow and broad facades, several overlapping roof profiles, restrained chimneys, shallow pitched roofs and parapets. A slightly larger central rear block anchors several lower neighbors; this should feel like a small city district surrounding an internal square, not one isolated corner building, two plain wall slabs, a narrow alley, or an asphalt intersection. Most building mass is along the two adjacent rear perimeter planes. Keep architecture as the main subject, with only small, low side returns near the front corners. Design low one- and two-story architecture with a planned visible boundary height around or below 6.5m; obtain a layered silhouette through variation rather than tall towers.
Exactly TWO exterior passages: one arched opening in each rear boundary, both clearly visible and connected to the central plaza via broad short stairs with clear landings. Left and right arch positions may be offset; repair one arch with an adapted metal structural brace so they are related but not identical. All remaining boundaries connect without third passage gaps, and other facade doors/shutters are visibly closed. Keep the stairs fully connected to the paving. No rooftop route that reads as an additional exit.
The broad continuous plaza center and both approach paths remain completely free of large objects, kiosks, vehicles, piles, pillars or machinery. Perimeter details stay in small clusters at building bases.

SALVAGE CYBERPUNK THROUGHOUT THE PLACE:
Architecture is long-used, partially failed and kept alive by recovered advanced technology. Show readable LARGE repair relationships: a substantial recovered transit power module with mismatched casing panels and an adapter cradle supplying several old facades through a few thick bypass cables; a damaged arch edge or roof support reinforced with a repurposed industrial coupling; an old machine casing repurposed into a modest utility awning or wall-service enclosure. Devices have worn painted housings, distinct joints and limited sensor/data features. Their bulky functional silhouettes should be noticeable relative to the buildings while remaining integrated at the perimeter. No randomly glued tiny sci-fi decorations, no new futuristic luxury storefronts, no wall-to-wall neon, no factory conversion. Old plaster, patched roof sections and faded replaced parts also convey decline away from the devices. Explain age through replacement and broad localized breaks, not dirty surface noise. Do not reuse the previous image's suspended cylindrical generator and arm under a bay window.

RENDERING AND DRY GROUND:
Render as a carefully simplified 3D GAME environment with crisp large forms, material boundaries and thicknesses suitable for later three-view design. Match the quiet wall and paving treatment of image 4. Leave most wall faces broad, uninterrupted, faded planes with a few large repair patches and selected large plaster losses near repairs. NO carpet of small chipped spots, cracks, pitting, grunge speckles, scratches or grain. Do not uniformly wear every edge. Preserve age and matte stone/plaster/painted-metal/cloth distinctions; do not turn buildings into pristine new plastic.
Paving is DRY broad stone slabs with subdued joints and a small number of larger replacement patches, larger and quieter than image 1's busy cobbles. NO water, puddles, wet sheen, glossy varnish, sparkling specks or mirror images of lamps/windows on the ground. Lamps produce soft diffuse light pools and coherent shadows on dry stone only. Keep the center visually calm as well as physically empty. No asphalt road, traffic stripes or road markings.
Cold desaturated slate-blue and silver moonlight, a few warm amber windows and repaired lamps, restrained faded ivory/charcoal and muted industrial color accents. Thin, low, broken translucent mist at building bases and partly across the ground; it never hides stairs, thresholds, ground direction or foundation edges. A small moon and faint distant city silhouettes can support the night, but the finite diorama remains unmistakable.

COMPOSITION AND FOUNDATION:
One complete standalone rectangular-base diorama, elevated three-quarter view, comfortably wide framing. Pull back sufficiently that ALL roofs, chimneys, outer wall ends and ALL base corners lie well INSIDE the canvas with clear dark-background margins. No clipped buildings, no panorama crop. Keep the two camera-facing sides open to view; low outer fragments must not hide the center or cutaway.
Both thick foundation cutaways expose broad earth/stone layers, structural footings and distinct useful infrastructure: one side a repaired drainage/pump manifold with a few thick connections, the other a protected power trunk and service branch feeding the perimeter buildings. Integrated closed utility panels only; no underground walking tunnels, cavern doors or third exit. Simplify soil into broad masses and a few large stones, not gravel texture.
No people, vehicles, logos, lettering, posters, signs, UI, split panels or multiple views.
Final image: a complete broad moonlit OLD CITY PLAZA with many readable building masses, two arched stair exits, quiet dry stone ground, visible salvaged technology, low mist, and both full ground cross-sections.
