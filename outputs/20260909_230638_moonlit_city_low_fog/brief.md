# 시가지 / 달빛 밤 / 낮은 지면 안개 / 건물 위주 — 테스트 1장

## 사용자 원문

좋아 지금 정도가 나쁘지 않아 기계의 비율은. 그러면 지금까지의 내용을 보완해서 한번 더 테스트 이미지를 만들어 보자. 그리고 괜찮으면 마스터 레퍼런스 내용으로 갱신할거야. 이번 키워드는 시가지 / 달빛이 비추는 밤 / 바닥에 안개가 낮게 깔려있음 / 건물 위주의 구성 / 한 장

## 적용 범위

- 이번 요청: 새 시가지 테스트 이미지 최종1장. 결과 평가 이후 사용자가 마스터 갱신 여부를 결정할 예정.
- 직전 출력의 건축 대비 기계요소 비중만 부분 선호로 기록. 구체적 기계/건물 복제나 전체 결과 승인이 아님.
- 원본 마스터01~04 v2, 실행v1.1 유지. 건물 형태가 중심이며 소수의 회수 센서·전력 장치·일상 설비와 기계적 단면으로 세계관 유지.
- 변화: 건물 위주의 새 배치/외관, 지붕·창의 큰 형태, 주광원이 되는 은청색 달빛, 바닥 일부를 낮게 흐르는 반투명 안개.
- 구조: 뒤쪽 인접두면+닫힌뒤코너, 각 면 출구1개, 바닥/건물/베이스 축 통일, 빈중앙과 두 접근로, 전체 프레이밍과 양면단면.
- 안개와 어둠이 문턱·중앙평면·건물하단·배관단면을 가리지 않도록 함.
- 미세 잡티 대신 큰 파손/수선/바랜색면으로 쇠락 표현. 밤/안개를 이유로 과도한 암부나 젖은 반사를 추가하지 않음.
- 구조 오류는 실제 검수 후 자동 수정·재생성 가능. 미관 취향만으로 자동 변형하지 않음.

## 실제 생성 지시

Create exactly ONE new game-environment concept image: an ARCHITECTURE-FOCUSED CITY BLOCK under MOONLIGHT AT NIGHT, with LOW GROUND FOG. The world is SALVAGE CYBERPUNK: an old, declining city still occupied through repair, reuse and surviving advanced technology. Use the input references only in their assigned roles.

REFERENCE ROLES:
1 = mandatory spatial topology. Two ADJACENT rear boundaries join in a solid rear corner, one separate exit through each rear face. No diagram colors, text or symbols.
2 = quarter-view camera, complete standalone base and two visible foundation cutaway sides only. Exclude neon, lettering, miniature-photo look and busy foreground objects.
3 = primary Little Devil Inside ARCHITECTURAL RENDERING treatment: purposeful large forms, quiet painted planes, selective wear and controlled detail. Exclude its characters, world, shops, plants and camera.
4 = subdued night atmosphere and localized lighting only. Do not copy the forest, natural surface detail, characters, equipment or darkness that hides the structure.
5 = USER-ACCEPTED AMOUNT OF MECHANICAL TECHNOLOGY RELATIVE TO ARCHITECTURE. Keep a comparable presence: a few readable wall-mounted devices, modest mechanized everyday objects and strong foundation pipework. Do not copy its buildings or exact equipment. This image is not a request to recolor the same alley. Design a new city location, while preserving the successful master topology.

STRUCTURE AND FRAMING:
A single standalone rectangular-base diorama seen in elevated quarter-view, slight perspective or near-orthographic. Frame the complete object with generous dark background margins on all sides; no outer wall, roof, passage frame, pipe or front base corner may touch or cross the canvas edge.
Two connected rows of compact aged buildings occupy the two REAR base edges, meeting in a CLOSED CORNER at the top of the image. Their facades face the open foreground. Create an unmistakable open passage THROUGH the rear-left row and another THROUGH the rear-right row, each with its whole frame, threshold and moonlit pavement beyond visible. Exactly TWO exits; all other ground-level doors are shut, shuttered or boarded. No central rear gap, no third passage.
Keep a modest but broad connected empty central movement/combat floor with clear approaches to both exits. All building footprints, pavement seams and base edges follow the same two axes on ONE continuous level ground plane. No rotated lane, wedge, floating tile, staircase or confusing elevation change.
Expose both camera-facing foundation sides: thick large earth/masonry sections, a small number of substantial pipes, repaired couplings and metal reinforcement plates. Mechanical foundation detail should remain as legible as in reference 5, now under soft cool fill light. Foreground remains open.

BUILDING-FOCUSED CITY DESIGN:
Architecture provides the main silhouette and visual mass: a few connected low old residential/workshop facades, varied shallow roof levels, a patched lean-to roof, boarded upper windows and broad plaster planes. Compact one- to two-level volumes with planned visible boundary heights at or below about 6.5m, including roof utilities; no towers or high-rise canyon. Keep the footprint contained rather than creating a vast plaza. Avoid converting the environment into a plant dominated by tanks or factory machinery.
Make decline and repair part of the building design: one large missing-plaster patch revealing simplified masonry, a short lost cornice repaired with a visibly different frame, a reused industrial access panel fitted into an old wall bay, a canvas patch on a weathered canopy. Retain broad intact faded surfaces between these selected large wear areas.
Integrate just a few functioning advanced remnants at the level accepted in reference 5. For example, one reclaimed sensor/relay assembly mounted on a mismatched bracket beside a closed utility doorway, and one substantial old power/data housing with a differently painted replacement cover and a clear bypass cable serving the opposite row. Each device has a readable body, large connector and repair relationship. Tiny dim cyan or amber indicators, not illuminated billboard displays. One or two small mechanically retrofitted refuse bins may sit at a wall with restrained bags or flattened packaging. These are secondary everyday fittings. No new collection of giant machines or central props.

MOONLIGHT AND FOG:
Clearly night. A silver-blue directional moonlight comes from above and one side, catching roofs, upper edges and patches of the pavement with broad soft-edged building shadows. Use cool desaturated ambient fill so the old wall planes, both passage interiors and BOTH foundation faces remain readable. A couple of weak warm windows or a small old practical light may suggest surviving habitation, but moonlight is dominant. The moon can remain outside the frame; no need for a landscape sky or huge moon.
Low, shallow, translucent ground fog drifts in broken soft bands across parts of the pavement. It hugs the floor rather than rising along walls. Make the effect visibly present, with floor seams still readable through and between wisps. Leave both exit thresholds and their approaches especially clear. Never obscure the front base edges, ground cutaway pipework, building feet or the entire central plane. No dense smoke wall, blanket cloud base or heavy haze that hides the geometry.

SURFACES AND STYLE:
Little Devil Inside-inspired restrained stylized 3D architecture. Coherent solids, thickness, contact shadows and distinct plaster/painted metal/cloth/stone/glass. Large aged color blocks, selective bends and repairs. Keep most wall and paving areas calm; many slabs have no wear marks at all. Age must read through construction, broad faded patches and repaired silhouettes rather than blanket grit. Reduce repeated small chips, scratches, rust spots, mottled tile stains and busy tiny reflections. Predominantly dry matte ground. No photoreal miniature, excessive depth of field, brushwork, toy plastic, extreme low-poly faceting, neon wash, detailed vegetation, people, vehicles, labels, readable lettering, arrows, HUD, split panels or multiple views.

One building-focused moonlit city diorama. Quiet low fog, readable aged high-tech repairs, the accepted modest mechanical ratio, EXACTLY TWO accessible rear exits, one coherent empty ground plane, whole base and two exposed mechanical foundation sides.
