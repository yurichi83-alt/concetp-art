## 실제 도구에 보낼 생성 지시

Use case: stylized-concept.
Produce exactly ONE game environment concept image using the five supplied reference images in their strictly separated roles. Create a newly designed urban shopping street in a declining post-apocalyptic SALVAGE CYBERPUNK world, at cold blue night. The defining impression is an old neighborhood keeping itself alive by repairing and repurposing the remains of a more advanced civilization. Its buildings and daily infrastructure must show prolonged decline and improvised maintenance at first glance, while its surface rendering remains composed, economical and cleanly readable.

REFERENCE ROLES:
Image 1: spatial topology ONLY — two connected rear boundaries, one accessible exit in each, open foreground and center. Ignore its text, arrows, icons and blue material.
Image 2: quarter-view camera, standalone full base and two visible ground cutaway sides ONLY. Ignore its neon, urban theme, miniature-photo finish, lettering and busy objects.
Images 3 and 5: the primary rendering treatment, based on Little Devil Inside's architectural forms, broad color fields, restrained mapped surface depiction and selective detail. Exclude its world, characters, plant detail, shop identities and camera. Preserve geometric/material clarity, NOT the intact or prosperous condition of its buildings.
Image 4: worn advanced technology and repaired material relationships ONLY — old painted metal bodies, exposed large functional joints, replacement parts, patched canvas covering. Translate these into buildings, service equipment and shop fittings. Do not insert its character, robot head or arm as decoration. Do not copy its illustration strokes or fine surface grunge.
Do not copy or preserve the previous generated shopping-street design. Establish this scene anew from the masters and the world story below.

COMPOSITION:
A single compact standalone rectangular-base quarter-view diorama, viewed diagonally from above with modest perspective. Show the entire base including its front tip, all footprint corners, rooflines and pole tops with comfortable dark atmospheric background margins. A connected L of low old shops and repaired boundary walls spans the two rear sides. Exactly two open passageways lead out, one in each rear side, both clearly visible and reachable from the central floor. Give the passageways distinct framing; close all other doors/shutters so they do not become extra exits. Preserve a broad continuous EMPTY combat plaza and clear approaches to both exits. Keep all tall or bulky props along building edges. Expose substantial masonry/earth foundation and a few large utility pipes on both camera-facing cutaway sides; no underground corridor. Keep buildings compact, with planned visible boundary heights around or below 6.5 m, without tall towers.

WORLD AT THE SCALE OF THE WHOLE PLACE:
The original buildings are old and damaged in a few LARGE readable ways, with repairs from different decades and sources. Show a partly lost cornice, a broad missing-plaster area exposing a few simplified masonry courses, and a damaged wall bay repaired with a reclaimed heavy industrial door mounted sideways inside a differently sized steel frame. Retain its obsolete hinge sockets so its former function is visible. These repairs completely close the boundary: no accidental passage through damage. Roof edges can sag slightly and have structural braces; avoid a pristine, evenly repeated shopping-center facade.
An operating repair/barter shop has an awning partly made from a reclaimed curved transport hull sheet, recognizable from its old service flange and blanked circular port. It is visibly cut to fit rough square supporting brackets. The remaining faded fabric awning is repaired with one large differently colored cloth section. Under it, a shallow perimeter counter contains a few sorted recovered actuator housings and spare battery packs, not shelves of identical new merchandise. This is an inhabited commercial street without people in this image.
A neighboring former shop is shuttered with mismatched reused flat plates and has an empty broken sign bracket. Another modest shop continues operating behind an old repaired window frame. Its fittings show different original materials adapted to current use; avoid uniformly matching science-fiction shells.
Make one legible infrastructure repair: an obsolete dead wall-mounted power/distribution housing has a missing cover and a capped old conduit; beside it a smaller recovered advanced power core sits in a crude, differently sized support cradle and connects around the failed section using ONE thick wall-hugging bypass cable with a clearly visible adapter. An intact portion of the old housing and the replacement machine remain visibly different shapes, paint ages and manufacture. A small dim cyan working indicator and a few nearby lamps explain current use. Keep the mechanical design large and functional, using several readable components rather than decorative greebles. Advanced technology is scarce, worn and adapted into everyday survival, not three new showpiece machines.
Two old utility poles stand at perimeter corners clear of exits and framing. One has a reused crossarm and a visibly different replacement electrical box, with a small number of sagging wires linking buildings. Some fixtures are unlit; the operating shops retain limited warm light within dominant cold blue-grey night ambience. Maintain legibility of both exits and foundation.
Appropriate trash: a few dented bins of differing ages, two or three bag/cardboard clusters beside shops, and a small neatly sorted pile of reusable metal offcuts at a wall. Keep disposal and reusable stock distinguishable by grouping. Center and exit routes remain clear. Old large paving units have one broad flush repair patch and a few selectively replaced slabs: aged but traversable, no rubble carpet or deep holes.

ART TREATMENT:
Little Devil Inside-inspired restrained game-3D architectural depiction: purposeful big silhouettes, readable thickness, broad quiet painted areas, subtle material response, clear cast shadows. Clean texture depiction does not mean new, straight or undamaged objects. Make decay through large bent edges, substantial patched sections, modest sagging, faded paint blocks and isolated dark oxidized areas on old iron. Surfaces between those selected marks stay quiet. Clearly separate plaster, painted metal, cloth, glass and stone. Use muted blue-grey, faded charcoal, old off-white and limited dull ochre/teal, with warm local work lights. Predominantly dry matte pavement with no busy glossy reflections. Enough diffuse cool light to see the age of the buildings, no blanket black shadows. Preserve solid coherent geometry suitable as a concept reference for later three-view development, but output only this one perspective scene.

No characters, vehicles blocking space, central stalls, extra exits, neon signs, readable words, labels, arrows, HUD, diagram graphics, captions, split panels or cropped base.
